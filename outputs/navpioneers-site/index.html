Ask a product question →
Does every navigation system include the head unit and bezel?

Yes. Navigation listings are supplied as complete refurbished head-unit systems with the vehicle-specific bezel or installation kit shown for that SKU.

What details do you need to verify vehicle fitment?

Please send your vehicle year, make, model, trim, factory A/C type, steering position, and a photo of the original dashboard when possible.

Which navigation configurations can I request?

Most navigation systems can be quoted in 2GB + 64GB or 4GB + 64GB configurations. Ask us for current availability for your selected part number.

Can I request dealer, fleet, or bulk pricing?

Yes. Use the inquiry form with your target quantity and product list. We will confirm pricing, lead time, and shipping options for your request.
